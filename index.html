Workflow

How DeshiDokan Works for Everyone Involved

The student agent network

Students with local roots, connecting producers to customers

Verified student agents from our founders’ university use their district knowledge and supplier connections to collect products and deliver them directly.

What an agent does

  • Accepts assigned orders based on product source, delivery area and availability.
  • Confirms product availability with the local producer.
  • Collects the product and delivers it directly to the customer.
  • Records delivery with a one-time code or proof of delivery.
  • Earns a commission, tracked in a clear earnings ledger with withdrawal requests.
